The Fund

A fund that puts communities in charge of capital.

Nobuntu is a mechanism for economic change. It brings people together to pool capital, knowledge and ownership, and gives communities the power to decide where that capital goes.

Join the fund How it is funded

Our purpose

Economies that people own, not just work in.

Communities hold knowledge, relationships and the experience of their own places. Nobuntu helps that knowledge guide capital towards locally owned enterprise and long-term, regenerative development.

That means investing in people as well as structures: the ability to listen, deliberate, work through differences and decide together. Lasting development depends on capability that stays within communities.

Why cooperation

Cooperation is the most practical economic tool we have.

When people pool what they have, they buy, build and decide with more power than they ever could alone. Nobuntu applies that idea to capital itself.

Pooled, not handed out

Everyone who joins adds to a shared fund. Value is built together and stays with the people who built it.

Decided together

Communities shape the priorities, enterprises and decisions that affect their lives, with clear and accountable governance.

Built to regenerate

Economic activity that restores the people and living systems it depends on, so prosperity lasts.

How the fund is sustained

2.5% of FORUS Digital revenue. In perpetuity.

FORUS Digital has committed a permanent share of its platform revenue to Nobuntu. That gives the fund something most funds never have: an income that keeps coming, long after the first contributions are made.

Founding contributions build the governance, financial controls, team and digital platform the fund needs to receive that revenue and put it to work responsibly. Every rand contributed now prepares a fund that keeps working.

The revenue commitment is in place. Revenue has not yet been received, and no distributions have been made.

Built on digital infrastructure

Its own platform, so every rand can be seen.

Nobuntu is taking a full, white-labelled instance of the FORUS Digital platform, run as the Nobuntu Digital Platform.

  • Contributions and participation to be recorded in one place
  • Programmes to be run and reported through the same system
  • Governance tools that support, never replace, accountable people
