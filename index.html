A fund for change. Led by women. Open to everyone.

Nobuntu pools capital, knowledge and ownership so communities can shape their own economy. With 2.5% of FORUS Digital revenue committed to the fund in perpetuity, what we build together keeps working.

Nobuntu

What the name carries

A woman's name, built on ubuntu: I am because we are.

The fund is named for a woman because women hold so much of the knowledge, care and enterprise that keep communities going. Nobuntu puts that experience at the head of the table, where decisions about capital are made.

Why women lead

How the fund works

Many people. One fund. Lasting change.

Contributions build the fund today. A permanent revenue share keeps it working. Communities decide where it goes.

You

People join

Individuals, companies and institutions join the fund with contributions, skills and networks.

2.5%

The fund grows

FORUS Digital has committed 2.5% of its revenue to Nobuntu in perpetuity, so the fund has income that keeps coming.

We

Communities lead

Capital moves towards community-owned enterprise and regenerative development, with women shaping the decisions.

The revenue commitment is in place. Revenue has not yet been received or distributed. See where we are.

Cooperation, reframed

Not charity. Shared progress.

Cooperation is not an old idea. It is the most practical way we know to build wealth that stays where it is made. Nobuntu brings people together to pool capital, knowledge and decisions, so everyone moves forward.

Where the fund goes

Four focus areas. One purpose.

Everything the fund does serves community-owned enterprise and regenerative development: economies that strengthen people and the places they live.

Lead focus

Women shaping capital

Women as investors, fund managers, trustees and community leaders.

Community ownership

Communities shaping the enterprises and decisions that affect their lives.

Brain Capital

Knowledge, skills, health and confidence: the capability people need to lead.

Dialogue and decisions

Facilitation that helps communities listen, decide and act together.
