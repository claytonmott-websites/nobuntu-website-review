Roadmap

From founding circle to lasting institution.

A settled direction, delivered in stages. We do not publish completion dates we cannot yet verify.

Done

Register the vehicle

Nobuntu South Africa NPC, the vehicle for founding contributions and building the institution.

Now

Build the foundations

Founding contributions, governance, conflict-of-interest and donor due-diligence policies, financial controls and the Nobuntu Digital Platform.

Next

Establish the fund

An independently governed Netherlands-based public-benefit foundation, with South African and Dutch legal, tax and banking advice.

Then

Open the pathways

Community grant and investment pathways, a facilitators network and the women's leadership pathway, each with its own approvals.

Governance and stewardship

How the revenue will be stewarded.

Clear responsibility

Defined roles and decision-making, so resources are managed for Nobuntu's purpose. The South African NPC and the future Netherlands fund keep separate decisions, accounts and records.

Sound controls

Financial controls, records and operating arrangements to receive, account for and manage the revenue responsibly.

Open reporting

Regular reports on contributions received, how they are used, milestones reached and what we learn, published here.

Questions

The fine print, plainly.

Is my contribution tax deductible?
Not yet. Nobuntu South Africa NPC does not currently hold PBO status or Section 18A approval, so we cannot issue tax certificates. We will tell participants if that changes.
Will I earn a return?
No. Joining the fund is participation in a public-benefit purpose. It is not an investment, and no financial returns are offered.
Has the FORUS Digital revenue been paid?
The 2.5% revenue commitment is in place. No revenue has been received or distributed yet. Founding contributions build the institution that will receive it.
Can my community apply for funding?
Not yet. Funding pathways will open once the fund's structures and approvals are in place. You can register interest on the Get Involved page and we will tell you when they open.
Why a Netherlands-based fund?
The long-term plan is an independently governed public-benefit foundation (a Dutch stichting). It has not yet been established and depends on professional advice and approvals.
