Before you contribute

Good to know.

Where does my contribution go?
To Nobuntu South Africa NPC (registration 2026/761071/08), to build the fund's governance, financial controls, digital platform and team.
Why is payment through BackaBuddy?
BackaBuddy is a South African platform that processes contributions securely and sends your receipt. Your contribution goes to Nobuntu.
Is it tax deductible?
Not yet. Nobuntu does not currently hold PBO status or Section 18A approval, so we cannot issue tax certificates.
Is this an investment?
No. Joining the fund is participation in a public-benefit purpose. No financial returns are offered.
Can I give in other ways?
Yes. You can attend a panel, lend your skills, partner as an organisation or help spread the word. See all the ways to take part.
